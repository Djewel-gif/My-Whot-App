Official Whot Rules
Whot! is an exciting card game where players race to get rid of all their cards by matching either the number or suit of the previous card played. Originating in England and later becoming especially popular in Nigeria, Whot! combines strategy, timing, and special cards to create a fast-paced and enjoyable game.
How to Play Whot!

Players and Cards

Whot! can be played by two or more players. The game uses a special deck containing five different suits and special Whot! cards.

Suits and Card Composition

A standard Whot! deck contains five suits. Each suit contains different numbered cards.

● Circles

1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12, 13 and 14

▲ Triangles

1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12, 13 and 14

✚ Crosses

1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 10, 11, 13 and 14

■ Squares

1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 10, 11, 13 and 14

★ Stars

1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7 and 8

Whot!

Special wild cards that can be used to change the suit in play.

Setup and Deal

Before the game begins, the cards are shuffled and each player receives a starting hand.

  1. Shuffle the cards thoroughly.
  2. Deal six cards to each player.
  3. Place the remaining cards face-down to form the draw pile.
  4. Turn one card face-up to begin the play pile.
  5. The player to the dealer's left starts the game.

Gameplay

Players take turns playing cards. A card can normally be played when it matches either the suit or the number of the card currently on top of the play pile.

1
Play a card that matches the number of the previous card.
2
Play a card that matches the suit of the previous card.
3
If neither is possible, use a Whot! card if you have one.
4
If you cannot make a valid play, draw a card from the draw pile.

Using a Whot! Card

The Whot! card is a special wild card. When a player plays a Whot! card, they choose the suit that the next player must follow.

★ Whot! Special Rule

After a Whot! card is played, the player chooses a suit. The next player must play a card from that suit or play another Whot! card.

Drawing Cards

If a player cannot make a valid move, they must draw a card from the draw pile.

  • Draw one card from the pile.
  • The player's turn ends after drawing.
  • The player waits until their next turn to play the newly drawn card.

If the draw pile becomes empty, the played cards can be reshuffled to create a new draw pile, while keeping the top card in play.

Scoring

A round ends when one player successfully gets rid of all the cards in their hand.

In the scoring version of Whot!, players receive penalty points for the cards remaining in their hands.

Circles, Triangles, Crosses and Squares Face value
Stars Double value
Whot! 20 points

Game Variations

Whot! can be played using different house rules depending on the players.

Quick Game

Players can skip scoring and simply play one round. The first player to get rid of all their cards wins.

Whot! Frequently Asked Questions

How many players can play Whot?

Whot! can be played by two or more players.

What is a Whot! card?

A Whot! card is a special wild card that allows the player to choose the suit for the next play.

What happens if I cannot play a card?

If you cannot make a valid play, you draw a card from the draw pile.

What happens when the draw pile runs out?

The played cards can be reshuffled to create a new draw pile, keeping the top card in play.

How do I win Whot?

In a quick game, the first player to get rid of all their cards wins. In a scoring game, the winner is determined by the agreed scoring rules.

Final Thoughts

Whot! is simple to learn but can become surprisingly strategic. Matching numbers and suits, knowing when to use special cards, and managing your hand carefully can make every round exciting.

Whether you are playing a quick game with friends or a longer scoring game, understanding the basic rules will help everyone enjoy Whot! to the fullest.
